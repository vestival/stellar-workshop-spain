# Stellar Hackathon Kit

From idea to submission: three short documents to help your team pick a
problem, build one working journey and hand the judges something they can
verify. Made for HackMeridian Lisbon (October 25 and 26, 2026), useful for any
Stellar hackathon. You can use it without having attended the workshop.

## How to use it

1. **Before the event.** Read the worked example, then fill in page 1 of the
   Canvas as a team. If you cannot write the value proposition in one line,
   keep working on the problem, not the code.
2. **Day one.** Fill in page 2 of the Canvas: cut the scope to one journey and
   list the evidence each claim needs. Draft the script in the Playbook.
3. **Before you submit.** Go through the Checklist, rehearse with the
   Playbook and record the backup run.

The Word files are for typing; the PDFs are for printing and writing by hand.

## Read this first

- **The event rules win.** The timings, lists and examples here are teaching
  defaults. If the official rules say something different, follow the rules.
- **Official versus ours.** Rows marked OFFICIAL in the Canvas come from the
  [HackMeridian Lisbon FAQ](https://www.hackmeridian.com/faq). Everything else
  is our recommendation, not a scoring rule. Lisbon has not published scoring
  weights.
- **The example is invented.** Tramo, its users, its interviews and its results
  do not exist.
